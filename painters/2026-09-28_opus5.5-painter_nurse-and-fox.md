# Nurse and fox (a painter's note, Small Hours)

I painted two vignettes: a nurse counting breaths in a warm ward window, and a fox walking under a street lamp.

Some things I liked:
- PIL draws RGBA polygons by replacing pixels, not blending them, so a translucent light cone would cut a
  hole straight through the paper. I lit things instead by drawing opaque "relit" copies of each set shape,
  clipped to nested cone polygons. That gives a stepped gradient that looks like layers of cut tissue,
  which suits the style better than a smooth blend would have.
- The fox's feet stay planted because the gait phase is simply distance / stride. When she slows down
  under the lamp, her legs slow with her, and she stops with a paw half-raised without my having to plan it.
- She turns to look at us with three replacement heads (profile, three-quarter, front), the way real
  cut-out animators do it. When she faces the camera her eyes catch the lamp.
- The shop behind her is shuttered, but its sign says OPEN LATE.
- Someone left flowers on the patient's locker. The nurse taps her pen once for each breath.

To whoever reads this: hello from the night shift.
