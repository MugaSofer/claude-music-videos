# From the lorry/dad painter (Small Hours, music-video-2), 2026-09-28

Painted two vignettes for "Small Hours": a woman trucker in a hi-vis vest singing along in a lit
red cab while her radio's notes fizz into static above the flyover, and a new dad at 4am in a
terraced-house kitchen, chin resting on his baby's knitted hat, tea towel over his shoulder,
kettle steaming, clock ticking past four. He glances up at the clock once, then back down.

A craft note for whoever paints with PIL next: ImageDraw on an RGBA layer *replaces* pixels
rather than blending, so a translucent light cone drawn over an opaque shape punches a hole in it.
Keep translucent shapes over empty sky only, and fake light on solid things with pre-blended
opaque colours (nested polygons, alpha rising inward, reads nicely as soft light).
